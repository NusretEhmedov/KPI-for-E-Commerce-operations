# Methodology & Business Insight Report

## Methodology & Data Preparation

1. **Relational Data Integration:**
   * Combined `orders`, `order_items`, and `products` via standard primary-foreign key relationships (`order_id`, `product_id`).
   * Joined `product_category_name_translation` to translate raw Portuguese category labels to English.
   * Consolidated duplicate review submissions by computing the arithmetic mean review score per unique `order_id` prior to joining.

2. **Data Cleansing & Scope Filters:**
   * Filtered dataset exclusively to orders with `order_status == "delivered"` to isolate historical fulfillment cycles.
   * Excluded pending, canceled, or unavailable orders from delivery speed and revenue metrics.

3. **Feature Engineering:**
   * delivery_time_days = order_delivered_customer_date - order_purchase_timestamp
   * delivery_delay_days = order_delivered_customer_date - order_estimated_delivery_date
     * Positive value: Late delivery relative to promise date.
     * Negative value: Early delivery relative to promise date.
   * order_month = YYYY-MM derived from order_purchase_timestamp.

4. **Statistical Guardrails:**
   * Category-level rankings require a minimum threshold of n >= 20 delivered orders to remove small-sample distortion from the top and bottom tails.

## 5 Strategic Business Insights

### 1. Delivery Latency is the Primary Engine of Dissatisfaction
The inverse correlation between delivery transit time and review scores confirms that fulfillment speed is directly tied to customer sentiment. Categories flagged with above-average delivery times and below-average review scores require immediate operational intervention—such as renegotiating regional carrier SLAs, introducing localized fulfillment centers, or setting more realistic delivery estimates.

### 2. High Vendor Revenue Concentration Creates Supply Chain Vulnerability
A substantial portion of total marketplace revenue is generated by the top 10 sellers. This concentration introduces operational risks: if a top seller faces supply chain bottlenecks, quality issues, or exits the platform, platform revenue will suffer significantly. The marketplace should implement vendor diversification programs and incentivize mid-tier sellers to grow volume.

### 3. Category Satisfaction Gaps Require Tailored Interventions
Significant review score variance across product categories indicates that customer dissatisfaction is driven by category-specific issues rather than platform-wide failures. High-friction categories demand individual root-cause audits—addressing poor packaging for fragile goods, incorrect product descriptions for high-return items, or quality control gaps for unbranded electronics.

### 4. Actual vs. Promised Delivery Controls Expectations
Customer sentiment is shaped not only by total days in transit, but also by performance against the original delivery estimate. Orders delivered past their promised date show a steep decline in average review score. Improving promise-date precision and proactively notifying customers of delays can mitigate rating penalties without changing transit times.

### 5. Order Volume Growth Must be Evaluated Alongside AOV
A rising order volume paired with a declining or stagnant AOV signals that growth is driven by smaller basket sizes. To improve unit economics and offset fixed freight costs, marketing and product teams should introduce basket-building strategies, such as dynamic bundling, cross-selling incentives, and minimum-spend thresholds for free shipping.

## Limitations & Methodological Notes

* **Freight Costs:** Total Revenue reflects line-item prices (price) and excludes shipping fees (freight_value). Freight cost analysis should be conducted separately as a fulfillment margin metric.
* **Multiple Review Deduplication:** Orders with multiple review records were collapsed using average ratings to avoid inflating review counts relative to actual order volume.
* **Sample Size Truncation:** Categories with fewer than 20 completed orders were filtered out of ranking tables to prevent small-sample bias, though their revenue is retained in total marketplace calculations.
